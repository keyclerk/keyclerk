## How to accept
1. Read this version. VDHome keeps every version it has published, with its SHA-256 hash.
2. In your first pull request or merge request, write on its own line:

       I accept the KeyClerk Contributor Licence Agreement version 0.2 for every Contribution I mark as submitted under it.

   VDHome keeps a copy of that statement, with your account, the date and the commit, and sends the copy to the
   e-mail address you contribute with.
3. End every commit message of a Contribution with the line `Contributor-Licence: KeyClerk-CLA-0.2`. A Contribution
   without that line, or sent through an issue or by e-mail, is not accepted until you add it.
