# Governance

- **Steward:** the organisation named in [NOTICE](NOTICE) holds the KeyClerk name and the release keys, and appoints
  maintainers.
- **Maintainers** ([MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)) decide what is merged and released. While there is one
  maintainer, the maintainer merges their own pull requests after CI; from the second maintainer on, every pull
  request needs an approval from someone other than its author.
- **Decisions** are recorded in the engine's decision records
  ([github.com/keyclerk/keyclerk/docs/decisions](https://github.com/keyclerk/keyclerk/tree/main/docs/decisions)). Anyone may propose one in an issue; the maintainer
  answers in writing.
- **Appeal:** open an issue that names the decision and why it should change; the maintainer responds in writing
  within 30 days.
- **A new maintainer** is proposed in an issue after sustained contributions and appointed by the steward.
- **Security** reports go privately, never to an issue: [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
