# 0004: KeyClerk 1.0 is the broker lifecycle, with its own requests, approvals and campaigns

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-10-22
- Decider: maintainer

## Context
A review from altitude found the design far ahead of what was proven against a real broker, and an adversarial
review of the whole stack found the reconciler unsafe for a pilot: it applied records without the engine's checks,
accepted unsigned records and approvals, could be told to remove everything by an empty records file, could skip
removals and logouts silently, used one broad service account, let anchors and group names be squatted, ignored trust
floors in the plan, and served a console without authentication.

## Decision
1. **Requests, approvals and certification campaigns are KeyClerk's core**, not delegated to a separate governance
   suite; such a suite stays an optional peer through federation, master per fact class.
2. **1.0 is the broker lifecycle:** joiners, movers and leavers; grants, leases and delegated groups; requests,
   approvals and campaigns; the reconciler hardened against every critical and high finding of that review; the Keycloak
   plugin and one more.
3. **Frozen until a user asks:** places, access paths, keys and pools, situation levels, physical access and the edge
   stay in the code with their tests, maintained but not extended.
4. **Next:** harden the reconciler against those findings before any further proof run or pilot.
